# Roadmap

## Next
- More message templates per language, reviewed by native speakers
- More languages: Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi and Gujarati
- More writing styles: numbers spoken as words ("two three four five"), OCR-style errors, mixed scripts inside one number
- More types: voter ID, driving licence, passport, vehicle registration, bank account numbers
- Ready-made adapters for more detectors

## Later
- Multi-turn conversations (a number given across two messages)
- Images of documents (fake KYC cards) for OCR pipelines
